Keyboard-first

Built for keyboard warriors.

Every action in Velocity is a command, and every command has a shortcut. Reach for the mouse when you want to, never because you have to.

Command palette

Press ⌘ ⇧ P. Do anything.

Type a few letters and run any command: split a tile, switch theme, move a note, toggle read mode. One list, one rhythm, thousands of keystrokes saved.

Quick open

Search that answers before you finish typing.

⌘ P searches every word of every note, with matches highlighted. ⌘ ↩ opens the result in a new tile.

    • Go to tab 1–9⌃⌥1…9

    Workspace

    A workspace that tiles itself.

    Split the editor into tiles and keep several notes in view. Pick the tiling that fits your head: classic dwindle splits, or a scrolling strip of columns.

    Dwindle

    Every new tile splits the one you are in.

    Automatic, along the longer side. Zero fiddling, zero dragging, and the layout comes back after a reload.

    • Split ⌃⌥\ right or ⌃⌥- down, or ⌘\ to let Velocity choose
    • Rotate a split ⌃⌥R, equalise ⌃⌥=, drag dividers to resize
    • Empty tiles are launchers: type, then press ↩
    Sliding

    An endless strip of columns.

    Inspired by niri and PaperWM. Columns sit on a horizontal strip that scrolls to keep your focus in view, so a tile is never squeezed to make room for another.

    • Toggle with ⌃⌥T. Cycle width ½, ⅔, full, ⅓ with ⌃⌥C
    • Stack tiles in a column with ⌃⌥-
    • Wheel or drag to pan, drag an edge to resize, jump from the minimap

    • Focus

      ⌃⌥ ←↑↓→ or H J K L

    • Swap

      Add ⇧ to carry a note to the neighbouring tile.

    • Zoom

      ⌃⌥↩ monocle any tile. Again to return.

    • Drag to tile

      Drop a note on a tile edge to split there, or in the centre to replace.

    • Tiles never reload

      Editors stay mounted: cursor, scroll and undo history survive every move.

    • Layout restore

      Tabs and tiles come back exactly as you left them.

    Workflow

    Write. Sketch. Stay organised.

    One place to think in words, in diagrams, and in folders, with nothing between you and the page.

    Writing

    A real editor, not a text box.

    Markdown renders as you type, and the keys you already know just work.

    • Live preview. Headings, emphasis, bullets, round checklists and images render inline.
    • Read mode ⌘E turns any note into typeset, sanitised Markdown.
    • Multi-cursor. Alt + click to edit in many places at once.
    • Find in note ⌘F, plus formatting on the keys: ⌘B ⌘I ⌘K.
    • Paste or drop images. They are stored right alongside your notes.
    • Your type, your way. System, Serif or Mono, with your own text size and line width.
    • Always aware. Live word and character count, and a gentle warning past 1 MB.
    Whiteboards

    Every note can think in pictures.

    A full Excalidraw canvas attached to any note, open it as a tile right beside your words.

    • One keystroke. ⌘⇧D opens the board as just another tile.
    • Synced. Draw on one device, pick it up on another. Boards live in your database, not in a browser.
    • Roomy. Scenes up to 10 MB, saved through the same retrying queue as your text.
    • Tidy. Delete a note and its board goes with it.
    Organisation

    Order that keeps itself.

    Flat, forgiving folders and a library that tidies up after you.

    • Folders, Unfiled last. Move a note from its row chip, the toolbar, or ⌃⌥M, with “New Folder…” always one click away.
    • Forgiving drag and drop. Drop on a folder header. Folders spring open as you hover.
    • Titles for free. Untitled notes take their name from the first line.
    • Instant search across every title and every word.
    • Auto-tidy. Blank, untitled, board-less notes are swept away. Recent ones are kept, and anything you touched in the last ten minutes is never deleted.

    Reliability

    Never lose a keystroke.

    Velocity is local-first. Your typing lands in memory instantly, and a patient background worker carries it to your disk. If the network drops, you will not notice. If the tab closes, nothing is lost.

    • Instant UI. Every interaction resolves on your machine first. Nothing blocks on a request.
    • Auto-save that never blinks. Changes are batched, sent as small diffs, and flushed when you close the tab.
    • Offline, gracefully. Retries back off, then notes are parked and resume on reconnect, on focus, or on ⌘S.
    • Self-hosted. One command and it runs on your hardware. Your data sits in a database on your own disk.
    • Markdown, always. Every note is mirrored to plain .md files, with images beside them, following renames and deletes.

    Technology

    Engineered like it matters.

    A handful of principles, held without compromise.

    Instant everything

    50 ms

    Typing, tiling, switching, searching. Interactions resolve before you can notice them, because nothing waits on a round trip.

    Local-first core

    Your device is the source of truth during every moment you work. The server stores. The interface thinks.

    A durable vault on your own disk

    A single database file you can back up, move and own, mirrored to plain Markdown you can read in anything.

    Search that answers before you finish typing

    The index is warmed at startup and never rebuilt cold, so results arrive in under ten milliseconds.

    ⌘

    One source of truth for every command

    Shortcuts, the palette, tooltips and the cheat sheet all read one registry. Add a command once and it exists everywhere.

    Tiles that never reload

    Tiles are positioned, not rebuilt. Split, swap, zoom and rotate while every editor keeps its cursor, scroll position and undo history.

    Designed with Apple Human Interface principles

    Semantic colour, vibrancy, quiet motion and generous targets. It feels native because it follows the same rules.

    Built on React, CodeMirror 6, Zustand, FlexSearch, Hono and SQLite.
