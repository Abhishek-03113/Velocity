# Brag Plan: Velocity

## What is this app?
Velocity is a fast, private, self-hosted notes app for people who live on the keyboard: a real Markdown editor, a workspace that tiles itself, whiteboards, instant search, six theme families, and now plugins, starting with Blog Reader.

## The angle
**Every feature is one keystroke away, so the film is cut on keystrokes.** Velocity's whole personality is "a command for everything", so each scene opens on the real shortcut that triggers it (⌘E, ⌘P, ⌃⌥\, ⌃⌥T, ⌘⇧D, ⌃⌥B). Big 3D keycaps press in sync with the real footage, with a key click on every press. The app's own tiling grammar becomes the motion language: the app window swings between left and right like a focused tile, and the "everything else" beat tiles itself dwindle-style, the way Velocity splits a workspace.

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
The landing headline, typed live: **"Think at the speed / of your keys."** It types character by character with real keypress sounds and an orange caret, and "keys." lands in orange italic. No logo yet. The sound of typing is the hook.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The app window rises out of the dark on the music's drop, already mid-edit: Markdown live preview, a checklist ticked, ⌘E flips to Read mode.
- ⌘P: full-text search finds "search index" across notes before the query is finished.
- ⌃⌥\ then ⌃⌥−: the workspace splits right, then down, swaps and zooms. Then ⌃⌥T: the same notes become a scrolling strip of columns (sliding mode).
- ⌘⇧D: a whiteboard tile opens beside the note and a flow gets sketched.
- **The plugin moment (longest beat):** "Introducing plugins" → **Blog Reader**. ⌃⌥B, paste a link, and the article lands as a clean Markdown note, open side by side with your own notes, and gets marked Read (blue Unread → green Read badge).
- Themes: Catppuccin → Everforest → Nord → Solarized → Apple → Gruvbox switch on the beat, and the theme names light up in sync.
- The workspace tiles itself into a wall: Folders, the shortcuts sheet, the phone layout.
- "Never lose a keystroke.": the status bar's Edited → Saving… → Saved, then Local-first · Self-hosted · Markdown on your disk.

## Outro / punchline
Logo icon + **Velocity** wordmark, then "Think at the speed of your keys." comes back, with **Get Velocity.** and a mono line: `self-hosted · local-first · keyboard-first`. Music settles under the final hold.

## User flow worth showing
1. Entry: open a note and write. Markdown renders live, checklists tick, ⌘E reads it.
2. Key action: find (⌘P), split (⌃⌥\), slide (⌃⌥T), sketch (⌘⇧D), all without the mouse.
3. Result: ⌃⌥B turns a blog URL into a Markdown note in the Blogs folder, read beside your notes and marked as Read.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished` (with `cinematic` scale at the drop and the outro)
- Creative direction: premium keynote-style launch film for a keyboard-first notes app; editorial serif statements, tactile keycaps, real footage in a floating window.
- Interpretation: confident and calm, never shouty. Big serif headlines with long enough holds; motion is smooth and physical (window swings, keycap presses), cut on the beat. Restraint in copy (one line per feature, verbatim from the site), richness in motion and sound.

## Duration: ~55s (user asked for at least 30 s and every major feature plus the plugin; 11 beats at ~4.4 s each fit this)

Strong-cue locks: **5.74 s** (app window lands on the drop), **28.13 s** (Blog Reader title), **49.96 s** (logo outro). - Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; bass RMS makes the warm glow behind the app window breathe and slightly lifts the window's rim light. No waveform visuals.
